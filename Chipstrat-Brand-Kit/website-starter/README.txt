CHIPSTRAT WEBSITE STARTER

Open index.html to preview the homepage. article.html is an article template.
The entire website-starter folder can be copied to any static host. No package
installation, build step, framework, analytics or external font service is needed.

Files: index.html, article.html, styles.css, script.js, assets/, fonts/.
The menu works on mobile. Publication links go to the existing chipstrat.com.
Subscriptions continue on Substack; this starter has no form backend or paywall.

To customize:
1. Edit homepage and article copy in the HTML files.
2. Connect research cards to real article pages when you add them.
3. Add your domain's absolute canonical and Open Graph URLs before deployment.
   assets/social-preview.png is the supplied default sharing image.
4. Keep font license files with redistributed fonts.
5. The shared design rules originate in design-kit/tokens.css and components.css.

For Sea & Poppy: set data-palette="sea-poppy" on the html element, then replace
the logo, mark, icons and social image in assets/ with their Sea & Poppy versions.
For a dark section use data-theme="dark" and the light/reversed logo.

The starter is a separate website template. It does not alter your live Substack
publication, connect to a CMS, import posts, or change your domain.
