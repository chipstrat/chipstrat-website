INDIVIDUAL ASSETS AT THE TOP LEVEL
Every Chipstrat-*.png and matching .svg is an individual, ready-to-use asset.
Chipstrat-Square-Logo.png: square chip-only logo, 2048 x 2048.
Chipstrat-X-Logo.png: high-resolution chip-only profile image, 2048 x 2048.
Chipstrat-X-Banner.png: CHIPSTRAT and research description, 1500 x 500.
Horizontal, stacked, transparent and platform-specific files are alongside them.
See INDIVIDUAL-ASSETS.txt and TOP-LEVEL-ASSETS.csv for the full file guide.
SVG masters are real vectors. High-resolution PNGs are rendered from those SVGs.
