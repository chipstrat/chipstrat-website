VECTOR AND PNG QUALITY
SVG files contain real paths and shapes, with no embedded bitmap or blur filter.
They remain sharp at any size. Every PNG here is freshly rendered from the SVG,
never enlarged from the previous 400px PNG. Use PNG for social uploads; use SVG
in websites, slides and design tools that support it. Enlarged previews of PNGs
can look softer because they have a fixed number of pixels.
